Our assumptions

  • $330 a month buys 250 operator minutes. That is the mid-tier plan at Ambs ($330 for 250), MAP Communications ($339 for 250), and PATLive ($349 for 200).
  • Every minute past 250 costs $1.50. Published overage rates run from $1.20 (Ambs) to $2.99 (PATLive). We use the middle.
  • A call takes 2.5 minutes of operator time. Vendors quote 2 minutes for message-taking and 3 for calls with scheduling.
  • Practice size sets call volume. A 1 to 3 provider practice sends its service about 100 calls a month, 4 to 10 providers about 300, larger or multi-site groups about 600, following Helpware's 2026 cost guide.
  • A front-desk hire costs about $65,700 a year. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics puts the May 2025 median wage for medical secretaries and administrative assistants at $45,930. Benefits, payroll taxes, and paid leave add another 30 percent of total compensation in private industry (BLS, March 2026), so the loaded cost is roughly $65,700, or $5,475 a month, before recruiting, training, and turnover. That buys about 40 hours a week during business hours.
  • Not included: setup fees ($50 to $85 at some services), HIPAA surcharges, holiday rates, and the 28-day billing cycles some services use, which add a thirteenth invoice each year. All of these push the real number higher.

Prices were collected from public pricing pages in September 2026 and may have changed. This is a planning estimate, not a quote from any vendor.

Why the calls matter more than the fee

A 2025 analysis of 7,000 calls across 22 practices found that 42 percent of after-hours calls went unanswered (notifyMD), and roughly four in ten patient calls arrive outside of 8 to 5. Each of those is a booking, a refill, or a patient deciding whether to stay. The cheapest service is the one that actually answers, verifies the patient, and books the visit.
