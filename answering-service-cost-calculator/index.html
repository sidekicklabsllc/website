How the estimate works

Minutes handled equals calls per month times average call length. On a per-minute plan, the monthly cost is the base fee plus any minutes beyond the included bucket at the overage rate. On a per-call plan it is the base fee plus calls times the per-call rate. The first-year figure multiplies the monthly cost by the number of billing cycles and adds the one-time setup fee. Savings compare the human service to the flat automated fee over the same period. Taxes, HIPAA surcharges, holiday rates, and minimum-billing rounding are not modeled, and in practice they push the human figure higher, not lower.

Why the calls matter more than the fee

A 2025 analysis of 7,000 calls across 22 practices found that 42 percent of after-hours calls went unanswered (notifyMD), and roughly four in ten patient calls arrive outside of 8 to 5. Each of those calls is a booking, a refill, or a patient deciding whether to stay. The cheapest service is the one that actually answers, verifies the patient, and books the visit.
