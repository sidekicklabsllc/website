How it works

Four steps, every call.

Samantha works with Samantha Hub from day one. Calendar sync and direct EMR integrations are available on request.

  1. 1

    A patient calls

    After hours, on a busy line, or on a callback list. Samantha answers on the first ring, every time.

  2. 2

    She verifies and listens

    Returning patients are recognized by caller ID and confirmed by name and date of birth. New callers are registered on the spot.

  3. 3

    She acts on your real systems

    Books, changes, or cancels against live availability, takes a message for staff, or tells an emergency caller to hang up and dial 911.

  4. 4

    Your staff gets the record

    Every call is logged in Samantha Hub, and a briefing of the night's calls is waiting when the office opens.

Safety & trust

Built for medicine, not adapted to it.

Healthcare calls carry protected health information. Samantha is engineered around that reality.

✓

HIPAA-minded architecture. Protected health information runs only on HIPAA-eligible, BAA-covered infrastructure. Non-compliant configurations refuse to start.

✓

Zero-PHI notifications. Staff notifications say only that a message exists. The content stays behind an authenticated console, so no patient information rides in a text message.

✓

Identity before information. No appointment details are shared or changed until the caller confirms their full name and date of birth.

✓

Redacted logs, bounded retention. Logs never reproduce caller speech or full phone numbers. Records are retained only as long as the practice requires.

✓

Honest by design. Samantha never claims an appointment is booked or cancelled unless the scheduling system confirmed it. When she is unsure, she says so and takes a message.

✓

Works with or without an EMR connection. Samantha Hub gives every practice scheduling, messages, and overnight briefings from day one. Calendar sync and direct EMR integrations are available on request.

For your staff

Get the Samantha app.

Practice staff can install Samantha on their phone for one-tap access to Samantha Hub: turn the line on or off, place calls, and read the overnight briefing. It installs from the browser - nothing from an app store.

Android

  1. Open app.samantha-medical.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Samantha appears on your home screen. Allow notifications when asked.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.samantha-medical.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Samantha from your home screen and allow notifications when asked.
