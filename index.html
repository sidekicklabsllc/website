How it works

Four steps, every call.

No scripts to write and no new software for your staff to learn. Samantha plugs into the calendar or EMR you already use and handles the call end to end.

  1. 1

    A patient calls

    After hours, on a busy line, or on a callback list. Samantha answers on the first ring, every time.

  2. 2

    She verifies and listens

    Returning patients are recognized by caller ID and confirmed by name and date of birth. New callers are registered on the spot.

  3. 3

    She acts on your real systems

    Books, changes, or cancels against live availability, takes a message for staff, or tells an emergency caller to hang up and dial 911.

  4. 4

    Your staff gets the record

    Every call is logged in Samantha Hub, and a briefing of the night's calls is waiting when the office opens.

What she does

A receptionist who never sleeps.

When your office closes, Samantha answers. When your front desk is busy, she takes the overflow and the outreach calls. Either way, your staff gets a clean record of every call.

📅

Real appointments, real calendar

Samantha books against real provider availability. She reads the appointment back before committing, never invents an opening, and never double-books. Bookings land in Samantha Hub for your front desk and, with a calendar connection, on the calendar your practice already runs on.

👋

Knows your patients

Returning patients are recognized by their verified caller ID. Identity is confirmed with name and date of birth before any appointment is discussed or changed. New callers without a chart are registered over the phone - name, date of birth, address, insurance - and scheduled in the same call.

💬

Messages that reach you

Callback requests, questions for staff, and refill requests to pass along are captured accurately and relayed to your team - with message content kept in Samantha Hub, never in the notification itself.

🚨

Emergency aware

Every call is screened for true emergencies before Samantha ever responds. If it's urgent, she directs the caller to 911 and, if you've set up on-call staff, brings them onto the line. She never triages and never schedules around an emergency.

🛡

Guardrails written in code

Booking caps, identity verification, rate limits, and data validation are enforced deterministically by software, not left to the judgment of a language model. Samantha proposes; the code disposes.

🩺

Stays in her lane

Samantha schedules and relays. She never gives medical advice, never interprets symptoms or results, and never makes clinical or billing decisions. Anything clinical goes to your staff.

📞

Outbound referral capture

Hand Samantha a stack of referrals and she calls each patient, introduces the practice, verifies them, registers them, and books their first visit. How referral outreach works.

🌅

Your morning briefing

Every call from the night lands in an authenticated briefing sorted by urgency, with the recording, a transcript, and a flag on anything that did not fully complete.

Text her too

Patients can text the practice line and get the same scheduling and message-taking, with staff notified the same zero-PHI way.

Reminder and follow-up calls

Samantha calls patients for reminders and follow-ups, verifies identity by date of birth before saying anything, and leaves a callback-only message if she reaches voicemail.

Safety & trust

Built for medicine, not adapted to it.

Healthcare calls carry protected health information, and Samantha is engineered around that reality from the first line of code.

✓

HIPAA-minded architecture. Protected health information is processed only on HIPAA-eligible, BAA-covered infrastructure, with a compliance posture that is verified in code at startup - a non-compliant configuration refuses to run.

✓

Zero-PHI notifications. Staff notifications say only that a message exists. The content stays behind an authenticated console, so no patient information rides in a text message.

✓

Identity before information. No appointment details are shared or changed until the caller confirms their full name and date of birth.

✓

Redacted logs, bounded retention. System logs never reproduce caller speech or full phone numbers. Records are retained only as long as the practice requires, with audit trails kept per HIPAA documentation rules.

✓

Honest by design. Samantha never claims an appointment is booked or cancelled unless the scheduling system confirmed it. When she is unsure, she says so and takes a message.

✓

Works with your EMR, or without one. Every practice gets Samantha Hub, a web portal with scheduling, messages, and the overnight briefing, so she works from day one, booking into the slots you hold open for her. Practices on Google Calendar or a calendar-sync partner get appointments on the calendar they already use. Direct EMR integrations are available on request.

Practice staff can install Samantha on their phone for one-tap access to Samantha Hub: turn the line on or off, place calls, and read the overnight briefing. It installs from the browser - nothing from an app store.

  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Samantha appears on your home screen. Allow notifications when asked.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.samantha-medical.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Samantha from your home screen and allow notifications when asked.
