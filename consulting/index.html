Consulting

The team that built Samantha, working on your problem.

Sidekick Labs LLC

Samantha is a production voice agent that answers a medical practice's phone: real telephony, real-time speech, a language model that books into a real EMR, and hard guardrails around every step because the calls carry protected health information. We built every layer of it ourselves. That experience is what we consult on, for two kinds of clients.

🏥

For medical practices

You are being pitched AI from every direction and need someone on your side who knows what is safe, what is real, and what will actually save your staff time.

  • Front-desk and phone workflow assessment: what to automate, what to leave alone
  • Vendor evaluation with a HIPAA lens: BAAs, where the data goes, what to ask
  • Custom automations on your EMR: referral outreach, reminders, intake, follow-ups
  • Setup and rollout of Samantha or another tool, with staff training
🔧

For teams building AI products

You are shipping a voice or conversational agent and want an engineer who has already hit the problems you are about to hit.

  • Voice agent design: pipeline architecture, latency, interruptions, and natural turn-taking
  • Telephony integration: inbound and outbound calling, transfers, recording
  • Language model integration: tool calling, prompt architecture, deterministic guardrails around the model
  • HIPAA-safe architecture: PHI boundaries, audit logging, redaction, retention
  • Testing agents that talk: offline gates plus live end-to-end call harnesses
