SMS Messaging Programs

Effective August 18, 2026

Sidekick Labs LLC operates Samantha, a virtual front desk assistant for medical practices. Samantha sends and receives SMS messages on the following number:

(360) 912-9011

Two messaging programs run on this number. Both are described below, including how you opt in, what messages to expect, message frequency, and how to stop at any time.

Program 1: Patient Messaging

What it is. Two-way SMS conversation between a patient and Samantha, the practice's virtual front desk assistant from Sidekick Labs. Samantha can collect a message or appointment request and relay it to the practice's staff. No medical advice is given over SMS.

How to opt in. Text the number above. Sending a text message to (360) 912-9011 is your opt-in to that conversation. Samantha's first reply includes the following disclosure:

Hi, this is Samantha, your virtual front desk assistant from Sidekick Labs. How can I help you today? Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Privacy: https://samantha-medical.com/privacy-policy/ Terms: https://samantha-medical.com/terms-and-conditions/

Message frequency. Varies by conversation. You will only receive messages in direct response to texts you send.

Program 2: Practice Staff Notifications

What it is. One-way SMS alerts sent to opted-in practice staff when Samantha takes a new patient message or completes a notable action (for example, booking or cancelling an appointment). Staff alerts never contain patient names, message content, or protected health information (PHI).

How to opt in. Staff members opt in directly with their practice through a verbal or written request. This is an in-person enrollment managed by the practice, not a public web sign-up. After opting in, staff receive the following confirmation message:

Thank you for opting in to Sidekick Labs practice notifications. Msg frequency varies (0-10/day). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Message frequency. Varies based on call volume; typically 0-10 messages per day.

Opt-Out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message from (360) 912-9011 to opt out. You will receive a final confirmation:

Sidekick Labs: You have successfully opted out of messages. You'll receive no further messages. Reply START to resume. Msg & data rates may apply.

Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message from (360) 912-9011 for support information. You will receive:

Sidekick Labs: Thank you for contacting Sidekick Labs. Email info@samantha-medical.com for support. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Samantha's SMS programs are available on all major U.S. carriers.

No Shared Data

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how information is handled.
