What is an AI medical receptionist and how does it work?

An AI medical receptionist is software that answers your practice's phone line and holds the conversation itself. Samantha listens to the caller, verifies who they are, reads real provider availability, books or changes the appointment, takes messages, and screens for emergencies. Every call is recorded and transcribed into a briefing for your front desk. She runs after hours by default and can take daytime overflow and outbound calls as well.

What happens if a caller has a medical emergency?

Every call is screened for a true emergency before Samantha responds to anything else. If the call is urgent she tells the caller to hang up and dial 911 and, if the practice has set up an on-call line, brings that person onto the call. She never triages, never judges how serious a symptom is, and never schedules around an emergency.

Will patients know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. Samantha introduces herself as the practice's virtual receptionist and never pretends to be a person. In our experience callers care far more about being answered immediately and getting their appointment booked than about who answered. The demo line is the fastest way to judge this for yourself.

Is Samantha HIPAA compliant? Will you sign a BAA?

Sidekick Labs LLC signs a Business Associate Agreement with every practice before live patient traffic. Protected health information is processed only on HIPAA-eligible infrastructure that is itself under a BAA. Staff notifications contain no PHI, logs never reproduce caller speech or full phone numbers, retention is bounded, and the compliance posture is verified in code at startup. A full walkthrough is on the HIPAA page.

Does Samantha give medical advice?

No. Samantha schedules and relays. She never gives medical advice, never interprets symptoms or test results, and never makes clinical or billing decisions. Anything clinical becomes a message for your staff.

How does Samantha verify who is calling?

Returning patients are recognized by verified caller ID, but no appointment detail is shared or changed until the caller confirms their full name and date of birth. New callers without a chart are registered over the phone with name, date of birth, address, and insurance.

Can she book, reschedule, and cancel appointments?

Yes. Samantha books only against real provider availability, reads the appointment back before committing, never invents an opening, and never double-books. Booking caps and rate limits are enforced by code, not by the language model. She never claims a booking succeeded unless the scheduling system confirmed it; if she is unsure she says so and takes a message.

Does it work with our EMR or calendar?

Every practice gets Samantha Hub, a web portal with scheduling, messages, and the overnight briefing, so Samantha works from day one by booking into the slots you hold open for her. Practices on Google Calendar get appointments on the calendar they already use. Direct EMR integrations are available on request, and we will tell you exactly what is live before you sign.

Can she handle after-hours and weekend calls?

That is her main job. Forward your line when the office closes and she answers every call until you open. She also takes daytime overflow when the front desk is busy and can place outbound reminder, follow-up, and referral calls.

What happens when Samantha cannot handle a call?

She takes a message and flags it. The morning briefing marks every call that did not fully complete so a person follows up. She does not improvise around a situation she was not built for.

Will an AI receptionist replace our front desk staff?

No. Samantha covers the hours no one is at the desk and the calls the desk cannot reach. Your staff start the day with a sorted briefing instead of a voicemail queue, and everything clinical or unusual still goes to them.

How is an AI receptionist different from a medical answering service?

A traditional answering service is a shared pool of live operators who take messages and page on-call. An AI receptionist answers instantly on a dedicated line, books appointments during the call, and delivers a recording and transcript rather than a typed summary. The full comparison is on its own page.

How long does setup take?

Samantha Hub works immediately, so a practice can be live as soon as the BAA is signed and the phone line is forwarded. Connecting Google Calendar adds a short setup step. We handle configuration on the intro call.

Can patients text the practice line?

Yes. Patients can text the practice line and get the same scheduling and message-taking, and staff are notified the same zero-PHI way.
